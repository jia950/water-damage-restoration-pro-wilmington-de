How to Prepare Your Delaware Home for Storm Season

Get your Wilmington, DE home storm-ready before the next big rain. Need help after a storm? Call our 24/7 crew now at (302) 648-0712.

Published October 29, 2026 · 4 min read

Storm season prep is worth the effort every year in Delaware. Wilmington and New Castle County see heavy rain, nor'easters and the remnants of tropical storms on a regular basis. Creeks and tidal rivers in the area have flooded before, so a little preparation before the next storm season can save a lot of cleanup and expense later.

Why Storm Season Prep Matters in Delaware

Delaware sits in a path that regularly catches the remnants of Atlantic storms, and New Castle County has seen major flooding events in recent years. Homes near low-lying areas, older storm drains or aging sump systems are especially exposed. Taking an afternoon each year to prepare your home for storm season is far less costly than dealing with flood damage afterward.

Outside the House

  • Clean gutters and extend downspouts away from the foundation
  • Check that the yard slopes away from the house, not toward it
  • Clear storm drains and window wells of leaves and debris
  • Check roof flashing and seals around windows and doors for gaps
  • Trim tree branches that could fall on the roof during high winds

Inside the House

  • Test your sump pump and consider a battery backup in case of a power outage
  • Lift valuables and storage boxes off basement floors onto shelving
  • Know where your main water shut-off and electrical panel are located
  • Take photos of your belongings and keep important papers in a waterproof container
  • Check that basement window wells have working covers

Build an Emergency Plan

Keep our number where you can find it quickly, charge phones before a storm arrives and keep a flashlight, batteries and basic supplies ready. Write down your main water shut-off location, your electrical panel location and a neighbor or family contact in case you need to evacuate briefly. If you lose power during a storm, a battery backup sump pump can be the difference between a dry basement and a basement flood cleanup call the next morning.

Document Your Home Before Storm Season Starts

Insurance claims move faster when you already have a record of your home before any damage happens. Walk through each room with your phone and take short videos of furniture, electronics and flooring, then store the files somewhere outside the house, such as cloud storage or email. A small amount of time spent documenting your home each spring can save real frustration if a storm does cause damage later in the season.

What to Do If Water Does Get In

If storm water does make it inside despite your preparation, stay out of flooded areas with live electricity nearby, and call for emergency water removal as soon as it is safe. For larger storm events affecting multiple rooms, flood damage restoration brings in the equipment needed to dry out an entire level of the home quickly, which lowers the risk of mold setting in later.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I start storm season prep?

Early spring, before the heaviest storm months, is ideal. Testing your sump pump and cleaning gutters at least once a year keeps your home ready year-round.

Is a battery backup sump pump worth it?

Yes, especially in Delaware where storms often knock out power at the same time heavy rain is falling. A backup keeps your pump running when you need it most.

What should I do first if a storm floods my home?

Stay away from standing water near outlets or the electrical panel, then call for emergency water extraction as soon as it is safe to limit how far the damage spreads.
