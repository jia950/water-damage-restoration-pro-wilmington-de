Protecting Your Home from Future Water Damage

Clean gutters and extend downspouts away from the foundation, test your sump pump before storm season, insulate exposed pipes before winter, and know where your main water shutoff is.

What to Do Right Now

Shut off the water source or main valve if it is safe. Turn off electricity to wet areas. Move furniture, rugs and valuables to dry spots, and take clear photos and video of the damage. Do not use household fans to push around sewage or flood water, and keep children and pets away.
