Signs of Water Damage in Your Home

Water damage is not always a puddle. Watch for these early warning signs and call as soon as you notice one.

Musty or damp smellA smell that does not fade often means moisture trapped in walls, carpet or under floors.
Stains on ceilings and wallsYellow, brown or dark patches can show a slow leak above or behind the surface.
Peeling or bubbling paintMoisture pushing out from the wall lifts paint and wallpaper.
Warped or buckled floorsWood and laminate swell and cup when water gets underneath.
Soft or sagging drywallWet drywall loses strength. A sagging ceiling can fall, so stay out of that room.
Visible mold spotsBlack, green or white spots can appear within days on materials that stay wet.
Condensation and damp spotsConstant moisture on windows, pipes or walls points to a humidity or leak problem.
Sudden jump in your water billA bill that rises with no change in use can mean a hidden leak.
