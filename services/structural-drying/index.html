Structural Drying in Wilmington, DE

Surface dry is not fully dry. Structural drying uses air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers to remove moisture hidden in framing, subfloors and wall cavities. Technicians record daily moisture readings and keep equipment running until materials reach normal dry standards.

Why Structural Drying Is Needed in Wilmington

After water is removed, moisture stays inside framing, subfloors, insulation and wall cavities. Structural drying uses air movers and dehumidifiers to pull that moisture out, which prevents mold, warping and odors. Professionals follow industry drying standards such as IICRC S500.

Signs You Need Structural Drying

  • Floors that feel damp or cup and warp
  • Persistent humidity after a leak
  • Paint bubbling or peeling

Our Structural Drying Process

  1. Moisture mapping with meters and thermal imaging
  2. Place air movers and dehumidifiers
  3. Check readings every day
  4. Remove equipment once dry goals are met

What Affects the Cost of Structural Drying

Drying costs depend on equipment count, days of drying, materials and the size of the affected area.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Turning the heat way up instead of using dehumidifiers
  • Removing equipment because the surface feels dry
  • Closing up walls before readings are normal
  • Using only box fans

When to Call

Call any time a wet area has not dried within 24 to 48 hours or you notice musty odors, warped floors or bubbling paint. Our 24/7 line is (302) 648-0712.

How Long Does Water Damage Restoration Take?

Restoration has stages. Water removal can be done in hours. Structural drying often takes several days, and longer for thick materials like hardwood, plaster or insulation. If walls, floors or finishes must be rebuilt, that comes after the structure is dry. Your contractor records daily moisture readings so you can see progress.

Wet Carpet After a Flood: Dry It or Replace It?

Carpet soaked by clean water can sometimes be dried in place if it is treated quickly, although the padding is often replaced. Carpet that touched flood water or sewage is usually discarded, because it can carry germs and is hard to clean. A moisture meter helps your contractor decide.

Signs of Hidden Water Damage Inside Walls and Floors

  • A musty smell that does not go away
  • Stains, discoloration or peeling paint
  • Warped, cupped or soft floors
  • Bubbling wallpaper or drywall
  • An unexplained rise in your water bill

Moisture meters and thermal cameras help crews find water you cannot see.

Structural Drying FAQs

How long does structural drying take?

Most jobs take three to five days, depending on materials and the extent of water.

Why is the equipment so loud?

Air movers and dehumidifiers run continuously to dry materials quickly and prevent mold.

How do you know when everything is dry?

Moisture meters compare wet areas to dry reference readings. Drying is complete when materials reach normal levels.

Will drying equipment raise my electric bill?

Yes, noticeably during the job.

How do you know when structural drying is finished?

The contractor takes moisture readings of walls and floors until they return to normal levels for each material, and records them.

Can walls dry without removing drywall?

Sometimes yes, if the water was clean and the drywall was not saturated. Heavily soaked or contaminated drywall is usually removed. The moisture readings guide the decision.
