Who We Are

Water Damage Restoration Pro is a referral network that matches Wilmington property owners with licensed, independent local restoration crews. We reduce the delay between a water emergency and the first technician on site.

How It Works

  1. Call (302) 648-0712 any time, day or night.
  2. A dispatcher listens to your situation and contacts a local contractor.
  3. The contractor arrives, assesses the damage and starts mitigation.
Transparent network notice: Water Damage Restoration Pro connects Wilmington property owners with licensed, independent local restoration contractors. Pricing and arrival times are set by the contractor.
